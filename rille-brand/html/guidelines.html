03 — corporate design color · type · rules
Color — black, paper, one signal
Ink#111111
Paper#F4F3EF
Line#D9D7D1
Graphite#5F5D58 · secondary text
Signal#FF4A1C · the dot, one CTA
Brand color is independent of the app UI. Signal appears once per layout at most — usually only as the dot in the mark.
Type — one family Geist 500 for wordmark & headlines (−4 to −5 % tracking), 400 for body. Geist Mono 128.00 Labels, BPM, keys, version numbers. Lowercase, no caps-lock shouting. Both open source (OFL) — free to ship inside a Linux package.
Mark & rules
A record seen from above, and the one point you drop the needle — the cue. Clear space = the dot's diameter ×2 on all sides. Min size 16 px.
— Lowercase wordmark only; never "RILLE" or "Rille". — Dot stays at 1–2 o'clock; never move, duplicate or animate it off the ring. — No gradients, shadows, outlines or extra colors. — Lots of empty space; one message per layout.
