01

Automatic beatgrids

Every track is analyzed once: exact tempo, bar start, key, loudness and a spectral waveform. Real doubts are flagged on the deck, not hidden.

02

Beat-locked sync

Synced decks follow the beat you hear from the master, not a BPM number — through loops, hotcue jumps, seeks and scratches.

03

Four decks

Play, cue, eight hotcues, auto loops from 1/32 to 32 beats, beatjump, flux, reverse, key lock and key shift on every deck.

04

Remix decks

Four slots of sixteen cells: capture loops from any deck or load one-shots, then trigger them quantized and in time.

05

Mixer

Gain with auto-gain, 3-band isolator EQ with full kill, DJ filter, crossfader, headphone cue and a master limiter.

06

FX

Two units with three slots — delay, reverb, LFO filter, flanger, gater and beatmasher, all tempo-synced.

07

Library & explorer

Music folders, tags and cover art, search, ratings, colors, playlists, history — and Traktor collection import.

08

Controllers

MIDI and HID mappings with LED feedback, MIDI learn, soft takeover and hotplug — remembered per device.

Grids you don't have to fix.

Grid lines sit on the start of the kick, measured from the average of all beats, so the tempo comes out exact — 144.000, not 144.002. Produced music gets a constant grid, tempo changes a piecewise one, live-played music a per-beat map.

Press TICK on a deck to hear a click on every beat, and check any grid by ear. If one is off, the grid editor fixes it in two clicks and remembers.

Built for Linux, not ported to it.

rille talks to PipeWire natively, or to JACK and ALSA. Headphone cue on outputs 3/4 of a four-channel interface, or split mono on two. A real-time audio thread that never allocates, locks or waits.

Running a hardware mixer like the Allen & Heath Xone:96? External mixer mode puts every deck on its own channel.

Bring your controller.

Mappings for these ship with rille. Anything else with MIDI works through MIDI learn — click a control on screen, move it on the hardware, done.

Get rille.

Builds from source in a few minutes and installs into ~/.local — no root needed.

$ git clone https://github.com/birkmann/rille-01.git rille
$ cd rille && ./scripts/install.sh
$ rille

Arch Linux

cd packaging/arch && makepkg -si — installs system-wide, including the udev rule for HID controllers.

Requirements

Rust (stable), Qt 6.5+ (qt6-base, qt6-declarative), clang, and PipeWire, JACK or ALSA development files.

Flatpak & AppImage

Recipes are in packaging/. Prebuilt packages will follow with the first release.
