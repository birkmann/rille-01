Features

01

Automatic beatgrids

Each track is analyzed once for tempo, bar start, key, loudness and waveform. Tracks with an uncertain grid are marked on the deck.

02

Sync

Synced decks stay in phase with the master deck, including after loops, hotcue jumps, seeks and scratches.

03

Four decks

Each deck has play, cue, eight hotcues, loops from 1/32 to 32 beats, beatjump, flux, reverse, key lock and key shift.

04

Remix decks

Four slots with sixteen cells each. Capture loops from any deck or load one-shots, and trigger them quantized.

05

Mixer

Gain with auto-gain, 3-band isolator EQ with full kill, DJ filter, crossfader, headphone cue and a master limiter.

06

FX

Two FX units with three slots each. Delay, reverb, LFO filter, flanger, gater and beatmasher, all tempo-synced.

07

Library & explorer

Music folders, tags, cover art, search, ratings, colors, playlists, history and Traktor collection import.

08

Controllers

MIDI and HID mappings with LED feedback, MIDI learn, soft takeover and hotplug. Settings are saved per device.

09

Stems

Each track can be split into drums, bass, other and vocals, each with its own level and mute. Separated stems are kept for the next time.

10

Recording

The main mix is recorded to a WAV file, with a cue sheet listing the tracks played.

Beatgrids

Grid lines are placed on the start of the kick, and the tempo is calculated from the average of all beats. Tracks with a steady tempo get a constant grid, tracks with tempo changes a piecewise grid, and live-played music a per-beat grid.

TICK on a deck plays a click on every beat to check the grid by ear. Grids can be corrected in the grid editor, and corrections are saved.

Audio

On macOS, rille plays through Core Audio; on Linux it supports PipeWire, JACK and ALSA. Headphone cue goes to outputs 3/4 of a four-channel interface, or to split mono on a two-channel one.

External mixer mode sends each deck to its own output channel, for use with a hardware mixer such as the Allen & Heath Xone:96.

new · beatport streaming

The Beatport catalog, on your decks

Sign in with your Beatport streaming subscription and the catalog opens in rille's browser. Search it, paste any beatport.com link to a track, release, chart, playlist, label, artist or genre, or open your own Beatport playlists.

A streamed track is analyzed like every other one: beatgrid, key and loudness. Your cues and grid corrections stay with it, even when the file has to be fetched again.

  • Lossless FLAC
  • AAC 256
  • Link paste
  • Your playlists
  • Local cache
  1. 01

    Sign in

    Under Settings → Beatport. rille keeps the sign-in token, never your password.

  2. 02

    Find

    Search from the browser's search box, paste a link, or open your playlists and the tracks you streamed before.

  3. 03

    Load

    The track plays as soon as its first seconds have arrived, while the rest downloads.

  4. 04

    Mix

    Sync, key lock, hotcues and loops work as with your own files. Tracks stay in a local cache up to the size you set.

Needs a Beatport streaming subscription; lossless FLAC needs the Professional plan. rille is an independent project, not a Beatport partner integration, and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Beatport.

Install

rille runs on macOS and Linux.

macOS

For Macs with Apple silicon, macOS 12 or later. Open the disk image and drag rille to Applications.

Download for macOS

rille is not notarized by Apple yet, so macOS blocks the first launch. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security and choose Open Anyway.

Linux

Build from source and install into ~/.local. No root access needed.

$ git clone https://github.com/birkmann/rille-01.git rille
$ cd rille && ./scripts/install.sh
$ rille

Arch Linux

cd packaging/arch && makepkg -si — installs system-wide, including the udev rule for HID controllers.

Flatpak & AppImage

Prebuilt Linux packages are attached to each release on GitHub. Recipes are in packaging/.

Build from source

Rust (stable), Qt 6.5+ and clang; on Linux also PipeWire, JACK or ALSA development files. On macOS, ./packaging/macos/build-app.sh builds the app.
